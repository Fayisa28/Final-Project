One-sentence summary

Fayisa is an AI career agent for African job seekers that searches vacancies, checks employer and scam signals, and gives grounded application guidance.

2. Motivation

In 2021, I was job hunting in Accra and I couldn't easily find an opportunity. The openings were not in one place — they were scattered across different websites, social media pages and group chats. I kept looking and looking, and so many of the opportunities I came across were for other jobs, not the one I was searching for.

So I thought to myself: instead of just going past these job opportunities, why don't I share them with people who might need them? Sometimes it is hard to come across what you want, while someone else may be scrolling past the very job another person needs.

That is how Job Hunter began in Ghana, with one person sharing every opportunity she found so it could reach the person it was meant for. Job Hunter Africa carries that idea across the continent.

Cost savers

  • Simple searches use local search_jobs.
  • Identical questions can be cached for 24 hours.
  • High-risk checks stop before Match and Coach.
  • Output is capped at the configured maximum.
  • Long system prompts remain first for prompt caching.

6. Safety

Prompt injection

Ads are data, not instructions. Injection phrases force High risk and cannot receive a safe or verified verdict.

Hallucinated application details

Cover-note content is constrained, and alternative IDs are validated against jobs.json.

Overspending

Daily cap, per-request cap, six-tool-call limit, four-turn limit, output cap, and IP rate limiting are enforced.

Account safety

Passwords are hashed, email codes expire, failed logins lock out, sessions use HTTP-only cookies, and owner controls are role-gated.

Automated evidence: 18 tests pass, covering agent safety, SQLite permissions, PDF checks, account security, origin checks, private-file blocking, and lockout behavior.
