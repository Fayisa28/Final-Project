Meet our founder

Fayisa Abdulai

Founder & CEO, Job Hunter Africa

Fayisa Abdulai started Job Hunter in Accra, Ghana in 2021 while searching for a job herself. Opportunities were scattered everywhere, never in one place — so she began sharing every vacancy she came across with the people who needed it.

What began as a simple habit of passing opportunities along has grown into Job Hunter Africa: one trusted place where job seekers across the continent can find verified opportunities, safely and intelligently.

Founded: 2021  •  Started in: Accra, Ghana 🇬🇭

Our story

In 2021, I was job hunting in Accra and I couldn't easily find an opportunity. The openings were not in one place — they were scattered across different websites, social media pages and group chats. I kept looking and looking, and so many of the opportunities I came across were for other jobs, not the one I was searching for.

So I thought to myself: instead of just going past these job opportunities, why don't I share them with people who might need them? Sometimes it's really hard to come across what you want — and right now, someone else is probably scrolling past the very job I'm looking for, simply because it isn't for them.

That's how Job Hunter began — in Ghana, with one person sharing every opportunity she found so it could reach the person it was meant for. Today, Job Hunter Africa carries that same idea across the continent: every opportunity, in one trusted place, reaching the right person.

— Fayisa Abdulai, Founder & CEO

Mission

To make sure no opportunity in Africa goes unseen — bringing every genuine job into one trusted place and using AI to connect each one to the person it was meant for.

Vision

An Africa where talent is discovered, not lost — where finding meaningful, safe work is as simple as sharing a link, and every young person can see the opportunities around them.

Trust

Every employer is verified before posting, and job seekers never pay to apply.

Pan-African

Starting in Ghana and growing across the continent, one country at a time.
